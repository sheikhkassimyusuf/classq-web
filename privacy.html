Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Replaces the versions of November 17 and April 4, 2026.

ClassQ ("the App") is a classroom question-and-answer app developed by Yusuf Sheikh-Kassim ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who else handles it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, your name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). You can also add a school email address.

Courses: The courses you create or join, your role in each (instructor, TA or student), and the sessions in them.

Questions, votes and replies: The questions students ask, the questions they upvote, and the written or spoken replies instructors and TAs post. Other students see questions without names; the course's instructors and TAs see who asked each one.

Audio recordings: If you ask a question by voice or an instructor records a reply, the recording is uploaded so it can be written out as text and, for replies, played back to the course.

Reports: If you report a question as not belonging in a course, we record that you reported it. The course's instructors and TAs are told how many students reported it, never who.

Your agreement to the AI services: The date and time you agreed, in the App, to your questions and recordings being processed by the AI services in section 3.

Reminders: The sessions you ask to be reminded about, and how long before each one.

Device information: A push notification token for each device you sign in on, and your notification settings, so we can tell you when your questions are answered, when sessions open, and similar.

Crash reports: If the App crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with technical details: the error, your device model, operating system and App version. A sample of App activity timings is also collected to find slow screens. These reports do not include your name, email or the content of your questions.

Purchases: If you subscribe to a plan, the App Store or Google Play tells us what you bought, when it renews, and whether it is active. We never see your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App: courses, sessions, questions, votes and replies
  • To screen questions for content that doesn't belong in class, and to group similar questions together
  • To write voice recordings out as text
  • To let instructors and TAs moderate their courses, including acting on reports
  • To send the push notifications you have turned on
  • To manage your subscription
  • To find and fix crashes and errors

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

3. Third-Party Services

These services process data on our behalf, only to provide the parts of the App described:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, where all of the data above is kept (hosted in the US)
  • Anthropic (Claude): AI screening and grouping of questions. It receives the words of each question and the other questions in the same session, with no names, emails or other identifiers.
  • Deepgram: transcription of voice recordings. It receives the recording only.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications (your device's push token and the notification text)
  • Sentry: crash and error reports, as described in section 1
  • Apple and Google: subscription purchases through the App Store and Google Play

Before your first question or voice recording is sent, the App tells you what goes to Anthropic and Deepgram and asks you to agree. If you don't agree, nothing is sent and you can't ask or record until you do.

4. Student Anonymity

  • Questions are anonymous to other students: students never see who asked a question
  • Instructors and TAs of the course can see which student asked each question, so they can follow up
  • Vote counts are visible, but nobody is shown who voted
  • Reports are never attributed to the student who made them

5. Data Retention

Your account data is kept while your account exists. Courses and their sessions, questions and replies are kept until the instructor deletes the course; a deleted course is permanently removed 30 days later. Notification records are removed after 30 days.

Deleting your account: In the App, go to Settings and choose "Delete my account". This permanently deletes your account, profile, enrollments, the questions and replies you wrote, your votes, reports, reminders, push tokens and subscription records. You can also request deletion at classq.tech/delete-account. An active subscription must be cancelled separately in the App Store or Google Play.

6. Data Security

  • Row-level security on database tables, so each person can read only what their courses allow
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Secure token-based sign-in, with the session stored in the device's secure storage
  • Recordings kept in storage that only signed-in users can reach

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (your name and emails can be changed in Settings)
  • Delete your account and data (section 5)
  • Receive a copy of your data
  • Withdraw your agreement to the AI services, which stops you asking or recording in the App

To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

ClassQ is intended for use in higher education (college and university). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will tell you about significant changes in the App or by email, and update the effective date above.
