Questions, answered.

Some of our instructors already pay for ClassQ.

The department's plan covers them as soon as they're added. The app then shows them how to turn off renewal of their own subscription: they keep what they've paid for, and the department's plan carries on after. Or they can keep their own plan and give the place back.

Do instructors need a school email?

Yes: they're added with their address at your school, and their ClassQ account has to use that address. Adjuncts with only a personal address? Write to us and we'll add them.

Does the person who buys it have to teach?

No. Admins manage the plan without taking a place. An admin who teaches adds their own email as an instructor.

Can we add instructors during the year?

Yes. Your admins add them in the app while there are places left. For more places, write to us: we add them and invoice the months left in the year.

What happens if we don't renew?

The plan stops covering instructors on its last day. Their courses, questions and answers stay, and they can keep teaching on a plan of their own.

What about student data?

Students see each other's questions without names; instructors and TAs see who asked. What we keep and who handles it is in our privacy policy. Send us your school's data agreement and we'll review it.
