Support

ClassQ, questions in class answered in order

Need help? Send us a message or email support@classq.tech, and we'll reply within 1 to 2 business days.

Include the email address on your ClassQ account and, if it's about a course, its join code or course number.

Common questions

  • I can't join a course. Use the join code your instructor shared: 6 or 8 letters and numbers, not the course number (like CS 301). If it's turned down, ask your instructor whether the course limits who can join, or is waiting to approve you.
  • My session isn't taking questions. A session opens at its start time and closes at its end; homework stays open until it's due. Before then it shows as upcoming, and afterwards you can still read its questions and answers.
  • I want a reminder before class. Open the upcoming session and tap Set under the tabs. Choose 5 minutes to a day before. On a weekly session, choose just that session or every session of the schedule.
  • I was invited as a TA or co-instructor. The invite is on your Home when you sign in with the email it was sent to. The instructor's plan covers each course's first TAs, so you can usually accept straight away. Co-instructors, and TAs past those places, need a plan of their own: choose one in Settings, then Plan, and accept the invite.
  • Running a poll. In a session, tap the options button at the top right, then Start a poll. Write the question and two to six answers. You see the results as they come in; students see them when you close it.
  • A question doesn't belong in class. Open it and tap Report this question. Your instructors are told, not who reported it, and they decide whether to hide it.
  • Voice questions aren't being written out. Check that ClassQ may use the microphone in your phone's settings, and that you're online while recording.
  • I'm not getting notifications. Check that notifications are on for ClassQ in your phone's settings, then in ClassQ go to Settings, then Notifications.
  • Managing a plan. Plans are billed through the App Store or Google Play. Change or cancel one in your phone's subscription settings, or in ClassQ under Settings, then Plan, then Manage.
