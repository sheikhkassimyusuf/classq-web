How a session runs.

Lectures, office hours, labs and homework are all sessions. Set your weekly schedule once and each one opens by itself.

  1. 9:45 AM

    A reminder, if you want one

    Get a reminder from 5 minutes to a day before, for one session or every week.

  2. 10:00 AM

    It opens on its own

    Everyone is told it's open. Students type a question or say it.

  3. During class

    The most asked rise to the top

    While typing, students see similar questions and can upvote one instead. Classmates never see who asked.

  4. 10:30 AM

    Answered in order

    Take questions from the top. Answer out loud, reply in writing or by voice, or skip. The class sees it straight away.

  5. 10:50 AM

    Check the room with a poll

    Ask a multiple-choice question and watch the answers come in. The class sees the results when you close it.

  6. After class

    Nothing is lost

    Students keep every question and answer. Instructors see everything still waiting in one list.

Questions, answered.

Do students have to pay?

No. Students join free with their instructor's code.

Do TAs need a plan?

Usually not. Your plan covers 3 TAs per course on Standard, or 7 on Pro. Extra TAs and co-instructors need their own plan, and Standard is enough. Invite them by email.

What counts toward the course limit?

Only courses running at the same time. A course stops counting when it ends, and past courses keep every question and answer.

How does ClassQ use AI?

It groups questions that mean the same thing, writes out voice questions and replies, and checks each question before it posts. It never sees names or emails, and everyone agrees first.

Can I run polls?

Yes, in any session. Write a question with 2 to 6 answers. You see results live, and students see them when you close the poll. Nobody's answer is shown by name.

Are questions anonymous?

To classmates, yes. Instructors and TAs can see who asked, so they can follow up.

Does it work for online and hybrid classes?

Yes. It runs on phones, so it works in a lecture hall, on Zoom or Teams, or in office hours.

What stops questions that don't belong?

AI checks every question before it posts. Students can report anything that gets through, and instructors decide whether to hide it.

Can I be reminded before class?

Yes, from 5 minutes to a day before, for one session or every week. You're also told when each session opens.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, in the App Store or Google Play. Your plan lasts until the end of the period you paid for.
