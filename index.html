How a session runs.

Lectures, office hours, labs and homework are sessions. Set the weekly schedule once and each one opens for questions on its own.

  1. 9:45 AM

    A reminder, if you want one

    Anyone in the course can ask to be reminded before a session starts, from 5 minutes to a day ahead. For a weekly session it covers every week.

  2. 10:00 AM

    It opens on its own

    The class is told it's open. Students type a question or say it, and voice questions are written out for them to check before sending.

  3. During class

    The most asked rise to the top

    As a student types, questions like theirs appear, so they upvote instead of asking again. Similar questions are grouped into one with a count. Classmates never see who asked.

  4. 10:30 AM

    Answered in order, seen by everyone

    Instructors and TAs take the queue from the top: answer out loud and mark it answered, reply in writing or by voice, or skip. The class sees each change straight away.

  5. After class

    Nothing is lost

    Students keep every question they asked or upvoted, with the answers. Instructors see whatever is still waiting, from every session and homework, in one list.

Questions, answered.

Do students have to pay?

No. Students and TAs join free with a join code from their instructor. Only instructors subscribe, for the courses they run.

Are questions anonymous?

To other students, yes: they never see who asked or who upvoted. The course's instructors and TAs can see who asked, so they can follow up.

Does it work for online and hybrid classes?

Yes. ClassQ runs on phones next to whatever else you use: a lecture hall, Zoom, Teams, or office hours.

What stops questions that don't belong?

Every question is checked before it posts, and inappropriate ones are turned away. Students can also report a question; instructors are told and can hide it or keep it.

Can I be reminded before class?

Yes. Open an upcoming session and set a reminder from 5 minutes to a day before. On a weekly session it applies to every week. You're also told when each session opens.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are billed through the App Store or Google Play and can be cancelled there. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
